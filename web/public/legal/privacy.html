Privacy Policy

At TezSend, we are committed to protecting your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing address.
  • KYC Documents: Identity proofs (e.g., PAN, Aadhaar, Passport) and supporting documents (Rental Agreements, Invoices) required for regulatory compliance.
  • Transaction Data: Payment amounts, recipient details, and dates.

2. How We Handle Payment Data

We partner with PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (e.g., Airpay) to process your transactions. We do not store your raw credit card numbers, CVV, or expiration dates on our servers. All payment data is encrypted and securely handled by our processing partners.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To facilitate and process your payments securely.
  • To comply with anti-money laundering (AML) and KYC regulations.
  • To communicate with you regarding your transactions, account status, or customer support inquiries.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with:

  • Payment processors and banking partners to complete your transaction.
  • Regulatory authorities or law enforcement if legally required.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS/HTTPS), firewalls, and secure data storage practices to protect your information from unauthorized access.
