Refund and Cancellation Policy

1. General Rule

Because TezSend facilitates payments to third parties (landlords and educational institutions), transactions are generally non-cancellable and non-refundable once the funds have been successfully settled to the recipient's bank account.

2. Failed Transactions

If your credit card is charged but the transaction fails on our platform or fails to route to the recipient:

  • The transaction will be automatically cancelled.
  • A full refund (including convenience fees) will be initiated back to the original payment method.
  • Refunds typically take 5-7 business days to reflect in your account, depending on your card issuer.

3. Duplicate Payments

If you accidentally process a duplicate payment for the same invoice/rent month, please contact our support team within 24 hours. If the funds have not yet been settled to the recipient, we will cancel the duplicate transaction and issue a refund. If the funds have already been settled, you must arrange a refund directly with the recipient (landlord/institution).

4. Chargebacks and Disputes

If you file a chargeback with your credit card issuer without a valid reason, your account will be suspended immediately. Fraudulent chargebacks will be disputed using your submitted KYC and transaction logs.
